# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The template is used by independent developers, studios, and small teams that need to launch distinctive landing pages for local businesses quickly. The resulting pages serve mobile-first visitors who are comparing a nearby business, looking for proof of trust, and trying to take one direct action such as starting a WhatsApp conversation, calling, booking, or visiting.

## Product Purpose

Turn the D2 Bike Shop landing page into a reusable white-label foundation without flattening its personality. Success means a new business can be launched by changing one configuration file and its assets while retaining strong hierarchy, credible local proof, accessible behavior, search-ready metadata, and an intentional visual point of view.

## Brand Personality

Bold, direct, and trustworthy. The experience should feel like a confident storefront run by real people: energetic enough to be memorable, clear enough to act on immediately, and flexible enough to express very different local brands.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic marketplace templates, interchangeable SaaS landing pages, glassmorphism, endless icon-card grids, timid neutral palettes, fake editorial luxury, decorative gradients, and visual effects that compete with the business itself. The template must never look like a cycling theme with different copy pasted over it.

## Design Principles

1. **Make the next action obvious.** Each viewport should help the visitor understand the offer, trust the business, or contact it.
2. **Show local credibility.** Real photography, reviews, address, hours, and specific services carry more weight than abstract claims.
3. **Separate identity from structure.** Brand tokens, content, assets, metadata, and contact details must be configurable without editing layout code.
4. **Keep a point of view.** White-label means adaptable, not anonymous; every preset should still feel designed.
5. **Earn every effect.** Motion, borders, and visual accents should reinforce hierarchy or interaction rather than decorate empty space.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA. Maintain readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic landmarks, descriptive alternative text, comfortable tap targets, resilient zoom and reflow, and a reduced-motion alternative. Content and actions must remain available when animation is disabled.
